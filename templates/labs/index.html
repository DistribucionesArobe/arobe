About the founder

Edgar Alejandro Robledo Beltrán

Director General of Distribuciones Arobe S.A.S. de C.V. · Aceromax — a construction-materials import and distribution business operating from Ciudad Victoria, Tamaulipas, Mexico.

Mexican and Spanish national. Licenciatura in Finance and M.Sc. in Management (iMSM) from the University of Illinois Gies College of Business. Prior experience in data analytics at Nielsen (marketing mix modeling).

Self-taught software builder. Everything shipped by Arobe Labs — from the WhatsApp SaaS to the legal-document AI to the 5,000-line sports engine — has been architected and implemented hands-on, using AI pair-programming as the force multiplier.
